**GSE190939: caloric restriction and feeding time (mouse liver).** Liver RNA-seq from male C57BL/6J mice aged 6 or 19 months, fed ad libitum (AL) or 30% calorie-restricted (CR). CR food was given either in one portion at the start of the day or night (CR-day-2h, CR-night-2h), spread over the 12 h light or dark phase (CR-day-12h, CR-night-12h), or spread across the full 24 h (CR-spread). Samples were collected every 4 h for 48 h in constant darkness (CT0–44), with biological duplicates. 288 samples in total. Acosta-Rodríguez et al., *Science* 2022 ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abk0297)). Data: [GEO GSE190939](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE190939).

The **timecourse** model is recommended for this dataset. In *Results plot*, try colouring by `Group_1` (feeding condition) and faceting by `Group_2` (age), or plot `Pred_Error` and `Theta` by condition.
